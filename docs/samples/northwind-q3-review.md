# Northwind Analytics — Q3 2026 Business Review

## Executive summary

- ARR reached **$12.4M**, up 18% quarter over quarter.
- Net revenue retention is **114%**; monthly logo churn fell to **1.8%**.
- NPS improved to **47** (up 6 points).
- Growth is led by mid-market; enterprise pipeline is up 32%.
- Decision requested: approve **$1.2M** of incremental Q4 budget to expand the
  self-serve motion, finish enterprise readiness, and open the EU region.

## Performance

| Metric | Q2 2026 | Q3 2026 | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| ARR | $10.5M | $12.4M | +18% |
| New ARR | $1.1M | $1.6M | +45% |
| Net revenue retention | 109% | 114% | +5 pts |
| Gross margin | 81% | 78% | -3 pts |
| CAC payback | 14 months | 12 months | -2 months |
| Monthly logo churn | 2.4% | 1.8% | -0.6 pts |
| NPS | 41 | 47 | +6 |

## What worked

- **Self-serve conversion** climbed from 4.4% to 6.1% after the trial
  onboarding rewrite.
- **Insights** was adopted by 38% of accounts within 60 days and produced
  $840K of expansion ARR.
- **Partner channel** sourced 22% of new logos, up from 11%.
- **Mid-market** added 41 net new accounts, the strongest quarter on record.

## What did not work

- **Enterprise cycle length** rose to 96 days (up 11 days). Security review is
  the bottleneck: 60% of late deals waited on a questionnaire or pen test.
- **EU revenue** was flat. The product is English-only and data residency is
  not available, which blocks several prospects.
- **Support first response** averaged 9 hours against a 4-hour target. CSAT
  stayed at 88%, but escalations increased.

## Customer signal

- "We replaced three dashboards with Northwind in the first month." — VP Data,
  mid-market SaaS
- "Onboarding took two weeks; we expected two days." — Analytics lead,
  enterprise retail
- "SSO was the blocker. Once that shipped, procurement moved." — CTO,
  enterprise logistics

## Q4 priorities

1. **Enterprise readiness** — ship SSO/SCIM, finish SOC 2 Type II, and build a
   reusable security questionnaire library.
2. **EU launch** — localize German and French, and add Frankfurt data
   residency.
3. **PLG expansion** — usage-based add-on pricing and in-product upgrade
   prompts for high-usage trials.
4. **Support** — hit a 4-hour first response and hire two support engineers.

## Risks

- **Customer concentration** — the top 10 customers are 31% of ARR.
- **Inference costs** rose 14% quarter over quarter, contributing to the
  3-point gross margin decline.
- **Hiring plan** depends on converting the enterprise pipeline at the current
  22% win rate.

## The ask

Approve **$1.2M** of incremental Q4 budget:

- $600K go-to-market: four enterprise account executives
- $350K EU launch: localization and Frankfurt residency
- $250K platform and security: SOC 2, SSO/SCIM, questionnaire library

**Target:** $15.5M ARR by the end of Q1 2027.
